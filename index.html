Why home projects go wrong

Most interiors don't fail at design. They fail between the drawing and the site.

A designer here, a carpenter there, a contractor who has never seen the drawings. Decisions get made on site, materials change halfway, and the finished room looks nothing like the picture you fell in love with.

1
Too many vendors, no one accountableYou end up managing carpenters, electricians and suppliers yourself.
2
Guessing from a 2D drawingIt's hard to judge colours, lighting and proportions on paper.
3
Timelines and costs that keep movingWithout a fixed scope, every week brings a new "extra".

Space Manager closes that gap. The team that designs your space is the team that plans, coordinates and oversees its execution, against a scope you've approved.

Do you only design, or also execute the work?

Both. You can work with us for design, or for design plus turnkey execution, where we coordinate materials, vendors and site work. We'll recommend the right scope after understanding your project.

How much will my interiors cost?

It depends on the area, scope and materials you choose. After the consultation we share an itemised estimate, so you can see exactly where the money goes before you commit.

Will I see the design before work starts?

Yes. Key spaces are designed in photorealistic 3D so you can review them and ask for changes before anything is built.

How long does a project take?

Timelines depend on the size and scope of the work. We agree a schedule with clear milestones before execution begins, so you know what happens when.

Which areas do you work in?

We're based in Dehradun. Share your project location in the form and our team will confirm how we can help.

Do you handle offices and commercial spaces?

Yes. We design and execute offices, chambers and business spaces, planned around workflow, brand image and the people who use them.
